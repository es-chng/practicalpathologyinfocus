## What this is

A single-author microjournal. Each article addresses one practical question
and separates the evidence, the recommendation and the author's own view.

## What this is not

Not a peer-reviewed journal. Articles are not a substitute for formal
guidance, local procedures or professional judgement.

## Corrections

Articles are dated. If an article contains a substantive error, a dated
correction is published stating what changed and why.
