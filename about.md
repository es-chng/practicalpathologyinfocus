## What this is

A single-author microjournal built with [Scholar Schema](https://github.com/es-chng/scholar-schema).  
Each article addresses one practical question and keeps the evidence, the recommendation and the author's own view clearly separated.

## What this is not

Not a peer-reviewed journal. Articles are not a substitute for formal guidance, local procedures or professional judgement.

## Corrections

Articles are dated. If an article contains a substantive error, a dated correction is published stating what changed and why.

## The publishing system

This site runs on Scholar Schema — a schema-driven system for short academic writing.  
Article structure is defined in YAML schemas; the same schemas drive the browser form, validation, web templates and PDF layout. Published schemas are locked so articles keep the structure they were published under.
